# Landscape background provenance

The 28 files in `images/weather/urban/` and `images/weather/countryside/` were newly generated for Solaris Atmosphere Scanner with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool. They are generic illustrative atmosphere scenes, not photographs of Paris, São Paulo, Salvador, or any other named location. No third-party photographer attribution is represented. No exclusive copyright or trademark clearance is asserted.

The urban set contains seven conditions × day/night with a low, geographically anonymous skyline. The countryside set contains the same matrix with fields, low ridges, and sparse trees. All scenes reserve quieter space on the left for the hero copy and avoid embedded text, logos, watermarks, famous landmarks, vehicles, people, and UI.

The existing coastal files remain in `images/weather/` and are not replaced. Use `landscape-manifest.json` and `landscape-selection.md` when wiring the new variants into the app.
